# Phase 5: architecture review (recorded 2026-10-10; findings only, nothing decided or fixed)

A senior architecture review of the design and the code, against `decisions.md`, `architecture.md` and `docs/analysis/`. Scope: unmet requirements, code that departs
from the design, weak architecture (coupling, module structure, layers), security (validation, authentication, authorization), scalability and performance, error
handling and edge cases, test gaps. Every finding names its evidence; "needs verification" marks what was not confirmed. Each fix is confirmed with the user first
(decision gate) and recorded here.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-10: Review recorded as information; no finding is accepted or scheduled yet.
- 2026-10-11: Finding 1 decided (user): the last company admin cannot delete the account (409, same rule as `RemoveMember`); for any other member, a Company listener on `UserAnonymized` releases the membership and reassigns the jobs as `ReassignJobsWhenMemberRemoved` does. **Coded 2026-10-11, not checked yet:** port `Shared/Application/Company/CompanyMemberDeletionGuard` (Company implements it in `LastCompanyAdminDeletionGuard`; an admin whose account is not active does not count, so two admins cannot both leave; checked inside the deletion transaction under the company lock), `ReleaseMemberOfAnonymizedAccount` and the queued `ReleaseMembershipWhenUserAnonymized` (also releases a last admin, the erasure cannot wait; announces `CompanyMemberRemoved` with the member as actor); tests in `tests/Feature/Company/MemberAccountDeletionTest.php`. Confirmed by the user 2026-10-11: only an active other admin counts (option A). Decided (user, 2026-10-11, option C with mechanism 1): a company left without an admin shows on the admin dashboard, in the existing `companies_queue` card (`without_admin` count and `without_admin_companies`, up to ten `{id, name}`), derived from the data; a company whose first company admin has a pending, unexpired invitation is not counted. Coded, tests in `DashboardTest`; no email.
- 2026-10-11: Finding 2 decided (user): reverse matching, one SQL query selects the immediate alerts whose criteria fit the live job, delivered in batches; an equivalence test keeps the alert criteria and the job search reader in step. Finding 3 decided (user): an explicit `$timeout` on every long job (alerts, newsletter, erasure, recommendations) below `retry_after`; the `bulk` queue gets `retry_after` 600 s with `$timeout` 540 s; `critical` and `default` stay at 90 s. Coded 2026-10-11: `retry_after` is per connection, so `bulk` has its own connection `bulk` (`QueueName::connection()`) and a second worker; the keyword is matched in SQL too (user chose the `keywords` column plus the job's searchable texts: title, company name, tag names), so the 200-match cap per word of the public search does not apply to alerts.
- 2026-10-11: Finding 5 decided (user): a daily scheduled command finds anonymised accounts whose data still exists in another context and sends the erasure again; every `Erase*WhenUserAnonymized` listener gets `failed()` that logs at error level. Finding 6 decided (user): no outbox; the reconciliation sweep of finding 5 is the safeguard, a lost audit or notification event is accepted as a small risk. Finding 5 coded 2026-10-11 (user chose receipts over per-context residue queries): each erasure use case is an `ErasureStep` (Shared port, tagged by its context) and leaves a receipt in `identity_erasure_receipts` (migration 000114) when it succeeds; `identity:reconcile-erasure` (daily 04:00) runs again every step without a receipt for accounts anonymised 1 hour to 90 days ago and logs at error level a step that fails again. A new step is repaired for the last 90 days of accounts the same way.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Evidence | Finding | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | `src/Identity/Application/Account/RequestAccountDeletion.php:45`; `src/Identity/Interface/Http/routes.php:44`; `src/Company/Infrastructure/CompanyServiceProvider.php:94-97` (no `UserAnonymized` listener) | A company member, **including the last company admin**, can delete their own account. This bypasses the "last company admin" rule of `RemoveMember`; memberships and job assignments keep pointing at an anonymised account and a company can be left without an admin. | Needs a design decision: refuse the deletion with 409 for the last company admin, or a Company listener that releases the membership and reassigns the jobs (as `ReassignJobsWhenMemberRemoved`). Tests. |
| 2 | High | `src/JobAlert/Application/NotifyOfLiveJob.php:40-52`; `src/JobAlert/Infrastructure/Listeners/NotifyAlertsOfLiveJob.php` | Every job that goes live runs **one search query per immediate alert on the platform**; at the target volume (200,000 job seekers × 5 alerts) one publication can mean about 1,000,000 queries in a single queued job. | Invert the match: select the alerts whose criteria fit the job in one SQL query, or split the work into one queued job per batch. |
| 3 | High | `config/queue.php:43` (`retry_after` 90); no `public int $timeout` anywhere in `src` | No job or listener sets a timeout. A job running longer than 90 s (finding 2, newsletter, erasure) is released again while still running: double execution and false retries. | A `$timeout` per long job below `retry_after`; tune `retry_after` per connection or for the `bulk` queue. |
| 4 | Medium | `src/Matching/Application/SendRecommendations.php:56-78` | About 7 queries per candidate, and `liveNear(province, 300)` is reloaded for every candidate of the same province; at 200,000 candidates more than a million queries, synchronously in the scheduler process. | Cache the pool per province within a run, load contacts, preferences and deliveries per batch, and split the run into queued batches. |
| 5 | Medium | The 11 `Erase*WhenUserAnonymized` listeners (e.g. `src/JobSeeker/Infrastructure/Listeners/EraseJobSeekerDataWhenUserAnonymized.php`) | The erasure of personal data across contexts relies only on queued listeners (3 tries), with no `failed()`, alert or reconciliation sweep. ## Top three priorities

1. Finding 1: close the self-deletion of the last company admin (needs a design decision).
2. Findings 2 and 3: SQL matching for the job alert fan-out and queue timeouts, before the import and production volume.
3. Finding 5 (with 6): guarantee that the erasure of personal data completes (reconciliation or outbox), for data-protection compliance.
